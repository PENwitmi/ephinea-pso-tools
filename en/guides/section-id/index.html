    Other things worth knowing

    • Your ID comes from the combination of class and name. You pick the class first, then the name; if you do not get the ID you wanted, /modsecid changes it once, while you are below level 20. At level 20 it is locked.
    • Only the host's Section ID decides drops — some of the commons and all of the rares. Your own ID does nothing when you join someone else's room.
    • The daily forecast gives a class/gender rare-rate bonus, but it caps at 5% and rotates every day. That is small next to the 25% weekly boost or Ragol Boost Road, so it is not a reason to pick a class.
    • The tables below are for Ultimate. Which IDs drop an item changes with difficulty (for example Fire Scepter: Agni does not drop in Ultimate at all — it comes from Pinkal and others in Normal through Very Hard).
    • An O does not tell you how often you will meet the source. If it is a rare enemy, that is a separate question — the 1/N figure is the chance per kill of that enemy and ignores how many spawn.
    • Yellowboze has a different kind of upside: more meseta, and a higher chance of percentages, in Episode 1 only.
    • ES and S-rank weapons are Challenge Mode rewards, so they have nothing to do with your ID.

    Common weapon type rates

    These are the odds that a common weapon that drops is of that type — not the odds of a weapon dropping at all. This is what matters if you want to find your own high-hit commons. Saber, Handgun and Cane are fixed at 13% for every ID, so they never differentiate.

    Exclusive to one ID

    Items that drop from exactly one ID in Ultimate. The per-ID Cards, DB's Sabers, Flowen's Swords and Heart of… items are left out, since every ID has one of each by design.
