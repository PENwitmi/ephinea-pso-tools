The anniversary rebuild is on hold

The rebuild announced for 2026 is not happening this year. The 11th anniversary runs in the same shape as before, from 12 August to 9 September. Maximum Attack E and Anniversary Badges both stay; what changed instead is how badges drop.

This year's event

The official thread put it this way: "due to unforeseen circumstances, we are repeating the former Anniversary Event format". The announced rebuild did not happen this year, and both Maximum Attack E and Anniversary Badges stay as they were.

Dates12 August – 9 September 2026
QuestsEleven Maximum Attack E quests (Forest, Caves, Mines, Ruins, Temple, Spaceship, CCA, Seabed, Tower, Crater, Desert) plus August Atrocity. Each runs 5–10 minutes and cannot be failed
BadgesBronze (all difficulties) / Silver (Very Hard and Ultimate) / Gold (Ultimate only) / Platinum (Ultimate only, extremely rare)
Where they dropAnywhere, not just in the event quests. Drop rate modifiers do not apply — the rate is the same however many players you run with
MilestonesUltimate clears are tallied, and the least-cleared quest sets the server's progress. Boosts unlocked this way apply everywhere, not only in the event quests

Rappies that are scared off instead of killed do not drop badges.

Changes from the 10th anniversary

The rollBadges are now independent of enemy drops — one enemy can drop both a normal item and a badge
Who gets oneRolled once per monster as a party drop, so everyone in the party gets the same badge (with Classic drops it goes to the floor instead)
Where it goesStraight into your character or shared bank if there is room, otherwise to the floor. Notifications can be toggled per character with /badgenotify
Spotting themBadges on the ground hover up and down so they stand out from other red boxes
MilestonesShuffled and expanded, aiming for bigger rewards per unlock
Gold shopTwo additions: Heart of Flight Fan (turns certain slicers into Flight Fan) and Blue Powder Coating (changes the colour of certain weapons)

The badge rate modifiers carry over from last year.

Three most-cleared quests−25%
only while at least 500 clears above the median

August Atrocity 1 / 2+25%, always

Type /badge in game to see which quests currently carry a modifier.

What the event quests are for

Badges drop wherever you hunt. Three things still only come from the event quests.

Event questsHunting anywhere else
Badges off enemies Yes Just the same
Badges for clearing Yes
Number and type depend on difficulty		 None
Badge rate modifier +25% on the bottom three
August Atrocity always +25%, top three −25%		 Does not apply
Milestones Only way to move them No progress

If all you want is badges, hunt wherever you like. Drop rate modifiers from party size do not apply either, so they pile up while you play solo as usual. The event quests are for the clear rewards, the +25%, and moving the milestones. Milestones follow the least-cleared quest, so all eleven have to be run fairly evenly for the number to move — and in return, every boost unlocked applies to everyone.

How many badges a clear awards is not stated in this year's announcement or on the wiki, beyond depending on difficulty.

Source: Ephinea forums, "11th Anniversary Event Thread" (12 Aug 2026)/Ephinea PSO Wiki — Anniversary event

What was announced

This is the shape described in the September 2025 announcement. It has not been implemented this year, and nothing says it has been dropped either. The removal of Maximum Attack E and of the Anniversary Badges were both stated outright as "no plans to change".

The response in that thread was mostly positive, with several posts thanking the staff for keeping the year-round Maximum Attack E. What several players asked for was that the name Platinum Badge be kept if the item works the same way. The worry raised was that levelling the difficulty might be taken far enough to make the quests dull, and there were requests to keep them runnable solo or in a small party.

Why they wanted to change it

Badges drop per quest clear. A faster lap therefore pays better, and Forest is the easiest area in the game. Staff pushed back against this three times over three years (see How it changed over the years). The 10th anniversary still ended like this.

Forest ran 5.6 times as often as Seabed, the lowest. The bottom eight bunching up between 25,000 and 36,000 looks like people running each just far enough for the milestones and stopping. Adjusting the payouts never broke the pull of the efficient quest. That is what the announced rebuild of the quests and the currency was for.

Badges you already have

The shop quest, "Anniversary Badge Shop", can be selected year-round, event or not. Its rules are unchanged.

10 Bronze→1 Silver
10 Silver→1 Gold
Gold✕cannot be traded up into Platinum
1 Platinum→your pick of Sealed J-Sword / Psycho Wand / Heaven Punisher / Handgun: Guld / Prophets of Motav / Sonic Doll

The Platinum list is the same six this year, and the weapons come with 0% in every category. The Bronze, Silver and Gold shops are unchanged too, apart from the two Gold additions.

How it changed over the years

The shape it has now was set in 2017. After that, the only things touched were how milestones counted, and whether badges carried over.

YearDatesWhat changed that year
20161st11 Aug – 1 Oct

Called "Ephinea Summer Event". Three badge types, run on Maximum Attack 4, no milestones yet. It was extended partway through, making it the only seven-week event.

20172nd13 Aug – 10 Sep

The current shape starts here. Maximum Attack E arrives, the Platinum Badge brings the count to four, and milestones are introduced. It has held ever since.

20183rd6 Aug – 3 Sep

Badges were year-numbered and replaced annually. Last year's could only be traded three-for-one, and anything older was dead. Photon Sphere went from 50 badges to 70.

20194th2 Aug – 6 Sep

Counting was tidied to Ultimate kills during the event only. Weekly rotating boosts were dropped in favour of running everything from the start.

20205th17 Aug – 14 Sep

The last year of year-numbered badges. Otherwise much the same as 2019.

20216th7 Aug – 4 Sep

Badges become permanent. The 5th Anniversary Badge was converted into the generic Anniversary Badge, and older year badges could no longer be traded. From here they carry over.

20227th6 Aug – 6 Sep

Weekly boosts went back to rotating. Milestones were "rebalanced and spaced out further".

20238th5 Aug – 5 Sep

First push-back. Counting moved to all Ultimate kills, so hunting outside the event quests also made progress.

20249th12 Aug – 9 Sep

Second push-back. Counting moved to the clear count of the least-cleared quest, so running one quest alone stopped helping.

202510th11 Aug – 8 Sep

Third push-back. The three most-cleared quests took a −25% badge rate, the three least-cleared got +25%. Forest still finished 5.6× ahead.

202611th12 Aug – 9 Sep

The announced rebuild is on hold. Badges now roll separately from enemy drops, come out the same for the whole party, and go straight to the bank. Two additions to the Gold shop.

Sources: the announcement thread for each year (2016/2017/2018/2019/2020/2021/2022/2026) and the Ephinea PSO Wiki
