The anniversary event is changing

Ephinea's anniversary event is being rebuilt from 2026. The staff announced it on 20 September 2025. This page covers what that announcement said, and what the event has actually been until now.

Before you read on

This page is mostly about an announcement, not a shipped feature. It dates from September 2025 and is not a final specification. Once the real event starts, the differences will be added here.

What was announced

The announcement closes like this: "things are subject to change, but the removal of Maximum Attack E and Anniversary Badges are not subject to change." Only those two are settled; the rest is a plan.

Maximum Attack E event quests removed settled

The quest series that has been the centrepiece for nine years is going away. However, the year-round versions stay — several posters thanked the staff for that, calling them masterpieces. August Atrocity also remains available during the event.

Anniversary Badges become unobtainable settled

Whatever you hold now is all there will ever be. That said, the exchange quest stays up even after the next event, so there is no rush to spend them.

This carries weight because badges became permanent in 2021. Before that they were year-numbered and expired annually.

A new quest series planned

"Maximum Attack E has lived in the spotlight for too long. After 9 years, it's time for something new!" — a series exclusive to the anniversary event is to be created.

The stated characteristic is that they will all be of similar difficulty. "There will not be an option to simply play the easiest area in the game for full event rewards," and, explicitly, "there will not be a quest entirely in Forest."

Tokens replace badges planned

The ranked currency — bronze, silver, gold — goes away. Each event quest awards its own colour of token instead. Buying from the shop requires several colours, which means playing all the event quests.

The examples given: an item ticket for 2 red plus 2 blue, an addslot for 1 green, a photon sphere for 70 of every colour. But tokens will be tradeable, so you can farm your favourite and trade for the rest. Names and numbers are placeholders.

A platinum equivalent remains planned

The announcement only says "there will still be an equivalent to the Anniversary Platinum Badge" — no name, no acquisition method. Several players argued that if it does the same job it should simply keep the name: it is historically endearing, and a piece of the old event would live on.

Milestones and boosts stay planned

"All the other features of the anniversary event will remain, such as the milestone system and extra boosts that players can earn."

Source: Ephinea — "Regarding Future Anniversary Events" (20 Sep 2025)

Why change it

The staff had already spent two years chipping away at the "just run the easy area" problem. In 2024 they changed how the counter worked; in 2025 they added modifiers that pushed badge rates up or down by how popular each quest was. It did not go away, and the final clear counts from the 10th anniversary show it plainly.

Milestones advanced on the least-cleared quest. So running Forest 142,197 times did nothing for them; progress was set by Seabed's 25,417. None of that surplus reached the rewards. "There will not be a quest entirely in Forest" reads as a direct answer to these numbers.

How it worked

This is the shape it held from 2017 to 2025. It starts in August and runs for four weeks. Of the parts below, the quests and the currency are the two being replaced.

Four badge types gone from 2026

Dropped by every enemy except scared Rappies, never from boxes. Bronze from Normal upward, silver from Very Hard upward, gold and platinum from Ultimate only.

They ignore drop-rate boosts — only boosts granted by the event itself apply. They stack to 99.

Maximum Attack E — 13 quests gone from 2026

Five to ten minutes a run, and they cannot be failed. Clearing one awards badges, the number and type depending on difficulty.

Episode 1 has Forest, Cave, Mine and Ruins; Episode 2 has Temple, Spaceship, CCA, Seabed and Tower; Episode 4 has Crater and Desert. Plus the two "August Atrocity" quests, which can spawn enemies from every area.

The badge shop

Exchange happens in the Episode 1 quest "Anniversary Badge Shop", and that quest can be selected year-round. Bronze buys music disks and addslots, silver buys materials and item tickets, gold buys photon spheres (95) and mag cells.

Badges trade up ten-for-one, but you cannot trade up to platinum. A single platinum buys your pick of Sealed J-Sword, Psycho Wand, Heaven Punisher, Handgun: Guld, Prophets of Motav or Sonic Doll.

Milestones and the weekly boosts

A server-wide counter; crossing a threshold unlocks a boost for everyone. In 2025 there were eleven tiers, from +50% EXP at 2,500 points through rare enemy, rare drop, photon drop, meseta and badge rates, ending at +1% hit chance at 20,000.

On top of that, the four boosts that normally rotate weekly are all active simultaneously for the duration.

Sources: Ephinea PSO Wiki — Anniversary event/Anniversary Badge Shop

The ten years

Ephinea went live in 2015, so the first event was in 2016. Reading each year's announcement, two turning points stand out: 2017, when the current format appeared, and 2021, when badges became permanent.

YearDatesWhat changed that year
20161st11 Aug – 1 Oct

Called the "Ephinea Summer Event". Three badge types — bronze, silver, gold — and the featured quests were the nine Maximum Attack 4 quests. No milestones yet. Extended mid-event, making it the only seven-week edition.

20172nd13 Aug – 10 Sep

The year the current format appeared. Maximum Attack E debuted, the Platinum Badge made it four types, and the milestone system arrived, counting kills in Maximum Attack E and Christmas Fiasco. That skeleton held for nine years.

20183rd6 Aug – 3 Sep

Badges were year-numbered and rotated annually. "2nd Anniv. Badges have been replaced by 3rd Anniv. Badges"; 1st-year badges became useless, old ones converting three-to-one. Photon Sphere went 50 → 70 gold. The Chef and The Badger debut.

20194th2 Aug – 6 Sep

Badges stayed year-numbered. The counter was tightened to Ultimate kills only, for the whole event. The weekly rotation stopped and all boosts were on from the start. The Hunter was reduced to joke items; The Scavenger arrived.

20205th17 Aug – 14 Sep

The last year of year-numbered badges. Otherwise much as the year before: all boosts from the start, Ultimate kills only.

20216th7 Aug – 4 Sep

Badges became permanent. "5th Anniversary Badges have been converted to generic 'Anniversary Badges', and previous year badges can no longer be traded up." From here on, what you hold carries over. Photon Sphere moved to 99 gold.

20227th6 Aug – 6 Sep

The weekly boosts went back to rotating normally. Milestones were "reviewed and stretched out for balance purposes". Maximum Attack E's meseta reward was halved. The Scavenger was removed; The Chef was announced as available that year only.

20238th5 Aug – 5 Sep

The counter changed to all kills in Ultimate, so progress no longer had to come from the event quests.

20249th12 Aug – 9 Sep

The counter switched from kills to quest clears, unlocking on the least-cleared quest. Grinding one favourite quest no longer moved the needle.

202510th11 Aug – 8 Sep

Badge rates were nudged by popularity: the top three quests (500+ clears above the median) took −25%, the bottom three got +25%, and August Atrocity was always +25%. /badge showed the current modifiers.

Sources: the announcement threads for 2016/2017/2018/2019/2020/2021/2022, and the Ephinea PSO Wiki

What actually happened

The 11th anniversary event (2026) has not been announced yet at the time of writing. If it follows the usual pattern it will start some time in August and run for four weeks. Once it does, the differences from the announcement will be added here.
