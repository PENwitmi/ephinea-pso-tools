The anniversary event is changing

Ephinea's anniversary event is being rebuilt from 2026. The staff announced it on 20 September 2025. This page covers what that announcement said, and what the event has actually been until now.

As of 11 August 2026

This page is mostly about an announcement, not a shipped feature. It dates from September 2025 and is not a final specification. The 11th anniversary event (2026) has not been announced yet. Once it starts, the differences from the announcement will be added here.

What was announced

The announcement closes like this: "things are subject to change, but the removal of Maximum Attack E and Anniversary Badges are not subject to change." So only two things are settled; the rest is a plan.

The Maximum Attack E event quests are being removed settled

The quest series that has been the centrepiece for nine years is going away. However, the year-round versions of Maximum Attack E stay — several posters in the thread thanked the staff for that, calling them masterpieces. August Atrocity also remains available during the event.

Anniversary Badges will no longer be obtainable settled

Whatever you hold now is all there will ever be. That said, the exchange quest stays up even after the next event, so there is no rush to spend them.

This carries weight because badges became permanent in 2021. Before that they were year-numbered and expired annually (see below).

A new quest series planned

"Maximum Attack E has lived in the spotlight for too long. After 9 years, it's time for something new!" — a quest series exclusive to the anniversary event is to be created.

The stated characteristic is that they will all be of similar difficulty. "There will not be an option to simply play the easiest area in the game for full event rewards," and, explicitly, "there will not be a quest entirely in Forest." The numbers in the next section show what that sentence is answering.

Tokens replace badges planned

The ranked currency — bronze, silver, gold — is going away. In its place, each event quest awards its own colour of token: one quest gives red, another blue, and so on.

Buying from the shop requires amassing several colours, which means playing all the event quests. The examples given are an item ticket for 2 red plus 2 blue, an addslot for 1 green, and a photon sphere for 70 of every colour.

However, tokens will be tradeable. You do not have to play every quest yourself; farm your favourite and trade for the rest. The names and the numbers are explicitly placeholders.

Something equivalent to the Platinum Badge remains planned

The announcement only says "there will still be an equivalent to the Anniversary Platinum Badge" — no name, no acquisition method. In the thread, several players argued that if it does the same job it should simply keep the name: it is historically endearing, and a piece of the old event would live on.

Milestones and the extra boosts stay planned

"All the other features of the anniversary event will remain, such as the milestone system and extra boosts that players can earn."

Source: Ephinea — "Regarding Future Anniversary Events" (20 Sep 2025)

Why change it

The staff had already spent two years chipping away at the "just run the easy area" problem. In 2024 they changed how the counter worked; in 2025 they added modifiers that pushed badge rates up or down according to how popular each quest was.

It did not go away, and the final clear counts from the 10th anniversary show it plainly.

Forest was run 5.6 times as often as Seabed at the bottom. But milestones advanced on the least-cleared quest, so none of that surplus reached the rewards. The pace was set by Seabed's 25,417.

What the event has been

This is the shape it held from 2017 to 2025. It starts in August and runs for four weeks. Of the parts below, the quests and the currency are the two being replaced in 2026.

Four badge types gone from 2026

Dropped by every enemy except scared Rappies, and never from boxes. Difficulty gates the type: bronze from Normal upward, silver from Very Hard upward, gold and platinum from Ultimate only.

Notably, they ignore drop-rate boosts — only boosts granted by the event itself apply. They stack to 99.

Maximum Attack E — 13 quests gone from 2026

Five to ten minutes a run, and they cannot be failed. Clearing one awards badges, with the number and type depending on difficulty.

Episode 1 has Forest, Cave, Mine and Ruins; Episode 2 has Temple, Spaceship, CCA, Seabed and Tower; Episode 4 has Crater and Desert. On top of those sit the two "August Atrocity" quests, which can spawn enemies from every area.

The badge shop

Exchange happens in the Episode 1 quest "Anniversary Badge Shop", and that quest can be selected year-round. Bronze buys music disks and addslots, silver buys materials, item tickets and paints, gold buys photon spheres (95) and mag cells.

Badges trade up ten-for-one (10 bronze to 1 silver, 10 silver to 1 gold), but you cannot trade up to platinum. A single platinum at "The Guide" buys your pick of Sealed J-Sword, Psycho Wand, Heaven Punisher, Handgun: Guld, Prophets of Motav or Sonic Doll.

Milestones

A server-wide counter; crossing a threshold unlocks a boost for everyone. In 2025 there were eleven tiers, opening with +50% EXP at 2,500 points and running through rare enemy, rare drop, photon drop, meseta and badge rates, ending at +1% hit chance at 20,000.

Those boosts apply outside the event quests too.

All four weekly boosts at once

The four boosts that normally rotate week by week are all active simultaneously for the duration of the event.

Sources: Ephinea PSO Wiki — Anniversary event/Anniversary Badge Shop

Every anniversary so far

Ephinea went live in 2015, so the first event was in 2016. Reading each year's announcement, two turning points stand out: 2017, when the current format appeared, and 2021, when badges became permanent.

2016 — 1st (11 Aug – 1 Oct)

It was called the "Ephinea Summer Event". There were three badge types — bronze, silver, gold — and the featured quests were the nine Maximum Attack 4 quests. No milestone system yet. It was extended mid-event, making it the only seven-week edition.

2017 — 2nd (13 Aug – 10 Sep) turning point

This is the year the current format appeared. The Maximum Attack E series debuted, the Platinum Badge was added to make four types, and the milestone system arrived. At the time it counted kills in Maximum Attack E and Christmas Fiasco. That skeleton then held for nine years.

2018 — 3rd (6 Aug – 3 Sep)

Badges were year-numbered and rotated annually. This year, "2nd Anniv. Badges have been replaced by 3rd Anniv. Badges", and 1st-year badges became useless; old ones converted at a lossy three-to-one. Photon Sphere went from 50 to 70 gold. Two NPCs debuted: The Chef, who buys enemy parts for badges, and The Badger, who trades badges upward.

2019 — 4th (2 Aug – 6 Sep)

Badges stayed year-numbered (3rd to 4th; 2nd and older useless). The milestone counter was tightened to Ultimate kills only, for the whole event. The weekly rotation stopped and all boosts were on from the start. The Hunter was reduced to joke items, while The Scavenger arrived with yellow-special weapons, and the Stealth Kit yielded a Stealth mag cell.

2020 — 5th (17 Aug – 14 Sep)

The last year of year-numbered badges (4th to 5th; 3rd and older useless, converting three-to-one). Otherwise much as the year before: all boosts from the start, Ultimate kills only.

2021 — 6th (7 Aug – 4 Sep) turning point

This is where badges became permanent. "5th Anniversary Badges have been converted to generic 'Anniversary Badges', and previous year badges can no longer be traded up." From here on, what you hold carries over. Photon Sphere moved to 99 gold. The platinum list included Lavis Cannon at the time.

2022 — 7th (6 Aug – 6 Sep)

The weekly boosts went back to rotating normally, dropping the "all on from the start" of previous years. The milestones were "reviewed and stretched out for balance purposes", with no minimum kill or HBR requirements. Maximum Attack E's meseta reward was halved. The Scavenger was removed, and The Chef was announced as available that year only.

2023 — 8th (5 Aug – 5 Sep)

The milestone counter changed to all kills in Ultimate, so progress no longer had to come from the event quests.

2024 — 9th (12 Aug – 9 Sep)

The counter switched from kills to quest clears, and milestones unlocked based on the least-cleared quest. Grinding one favourite quest no longer moved the needle.

2025 — 10th (11 Aug – 8 Sep)

On top of that, badge rates were nudged by popularity. The top three quests — at least 500 clears above the median — took −25% badge rate, the bottom three got +25%, and August Atrocity was always +25%. Typing /badge in game showed the current modifiers.

Sources: the announcement threads for 2016/2017/2018/2019/2020/2021/2022, and the Ephinea PSO Wiki
