The anniversary event is being rebuilt

Ephinea's anniversary event has kept the same shape for nine years, and it is being rebuilt from 2026. The reason: farming Forest over and over was always the best move, and three years of adjustments failed to change that.

What is settled

Only these two were stated outright as "no plans to change".

The event version of Maximum Attack E is going

It was the centre of the event for nine years. The year-round version stays. "August Atrocity" also remains playable during the event.

Anniversary Badges will no longer drop

The next event is the last chance to earn them. The shop stays, so there is no hurry to spend what you have left.

Everything else is advance notice. A maintenance to start the event has been announced for 12 August 2026, 04:30 UTC, and may take up to an hour. Nothing about its contents has been published yet. Once it starts, the differences from the notice will be added here.

Source: Ephinea forums, "Server maintenance scheduled for August 12th, 2026 at 04:30 UTC" (12 Aug 2026)

The response in the thread was mostly positive, with several posts thanking the staff for keeping the year-round Maximum Attack E. What several players asked for was that the name Platinum Badge be kept if the item works the same way. The worry raised was that levelling the difficulty might be taken far enough to make the quests dull, and there were requests to keep them runnable solo or in a small party.

Source: Ephinea announcement, "Regarding Future Anniversary Events" (2025-09-20)

Why it is changing

Badges drop per quest clear. A faster lap therefore pays better, and Forest is the easiest area in the game. Staff pushed back against this three times over three years (see Nine years of changes). The 10th anniversary still ended like this.

Forest ran 5.6 times as often as Seabed, the lowest. The bottom eight bunching up between 25,000 and 36,000 looks like people running each just far enough for the milestones and stopping. Adjusting the payouts never broke the pull of the efficient quest. So 2026 replaces the quests and the currency themselves.

Badges you already have

Nine years of changes

The shape it has now was set in 2017. After that, the only things touched were how milestones counted, and whether badges carried over.

YearDatesWhat changed that year
20161st11 Aug – 1 Oct

Called "Ephinea Summer Event". Three badge types, run on Maximum Attack 4, no milestones yet. It was extended partway through, making it the only seven-week event.

20172nd13 Aug – 10 Sep

The current shape starts here. Maximum Attack E arrives, the Platinum Badge brings the count to four, and milestones are introduced. This held for nine years.

20183rd6 Aug – 3 Sep

Badges were year-numbered and replaced annually. Last year's could only be traded three-for-one, and anything older was dead. Photon Sphere went from 50 badges to 70.

20194th2 Aug – 6 Sep

Counting was tidied to Ultimate kills during the event only. Weekly rotating boosts were dropped in favour of running everything from the start.

20205th17 Aug – 14 Sep

The last year of year-numbered badges. Otherwise much the same as 2019.

20216th7 Aug – 4 Sep

Badges become permanent. The 5th Anniversary Badge was converted into the generic Anniversary Badge, and older year badges could no longer be traded. From here they carry over.

20227th6 Aug – 6 Sep

Weekly boosts went back to rotating. Milestones were "rebalanced and spaced out further".

20238th5 Aug – 5 Sep

First push-back. Counting moved to all Ultimate kills, so hunting outside the event quests also made progress.

20249th12 Aug – 9 Sep

Second push-back. Counting moved to the clear count of the least-cleared quest, so running one quest alone stopped helping.

202510th11 Aug – 8 Sep

Third push-back. The three most-cleared quests took a −25% badge rate, the three least-cleared got +25%. Forest still finished 5.6× ahead.

Sources: the announcement thread for each year (2016/2017/2018/2019/2020/2021/2022) and the Ephinea PSO Wiki
