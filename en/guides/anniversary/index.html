The anniversary event is changing

Ephinea's anniversary event has had the same shape for nine years, and from 2026 it is being rebuilt. The reason is not subtle: the staff spent three years trying to fix a structure where grinding the easiest quest was always the right answer, and it did not work.

What is settled

Of everything in the 20 September 2025 announcement, only two things were marked as not subject to change.

The Maximum Attack E event quests are going away

The series that has been the centrepiece for nine years disappears. The year-round versions stay, though, and August Atrocity remains available during the event.

Anniversary Badges become unobtainable

What you hold now is the last stock there will be. The exchange shop stays open, so there is no need to rush to spend them.

Everything else is a plan and may change. The 11th anniversary event itself has not been announced at all as of 11 August 2026, when this was written. If it follows the usual pattern it will start some time in August and run four weeks. Once it does, the differences from the announcement will be added here.

Why it is ending

Badges are awarded every time you clear a quest. That means the faster and safer a run is, the more you earn per hour. And Forest is the easiest area in the game.

Put those two facts together and the answer falls out: run Forest, over and over. It has been that way since the format was created nine years ago.

The staff spent three years trying to break it. In 2023 the milestone counter changed to all Ultimate kills. In 2024 it changed again, to the number of clears of the least-cleared quest, so grinding Forest alone stopped advancing anything server-wide. In 2025 they added rate modifiers on top: −25% badges for the three most-cleared quests, +25% for the three least-cleared.

Forest to Seabed is a 5.6× gap. The bottom eight bunching between 25,000 and 36,000 looks like exactly what it is: people running each of those the minimum needed for the milestones. Adjusting how the rewards were handed out did not stop players converging on the most efficient quest.

So for 2026, instead of adjusting the payout, they are removing the thing everyone converges on.

What replaces it

The new quest series is to be of uniform difficulty. "There will not be an option to simply play the easiest area in the game for full event rewards," and, explicitly, "there will not be a quest entirely in Forest." In other words: do not build the optimal quest in the first place.

The currency points the same way. The bronze/silver/gold ranking goes away, replaced by a different colour of token per quest. Buying from the shop needs several colours, so repeating one quest buys you nothing. The examples given are an item ticket for 2 red plus 2 blue, and a photon sphere for 70 of every colour. Names and numbers are placeholders.

Tokens will be tradeable, though. You do not have to run everything yourself: farm what you are good at and trade for the other colours. The "play everything" requirement is softened right there.

Something equivalent to the Platinum Badge remains, but its name and how you get it are undecided. Several posters argued that if it does the same job it should simply keep the name, since it is historically endearing and would let a piece of the old event live on. Milestones and the extra boosts carry over unchanged.

The reception was broadly positive. Plenty of posts thanked the staff for keeping the year-round Maximum Attack E quests, alongside two recurring worries: that chasing uniform difficulty might make the quests dull, and that they should still be runnable solo or in small groups.

Source: Ephinea — "Regarding Future Anniversary Events" (20 Sep 2025)

What to do with the badges you have

The coming event is the last chance to earn badges. The exchange quest, "Anniversary Badge Shop", remains afterwards, and since it can be selected year-round, whatever you have left can be spent whenever you like.

The trade-up rules are unchanged: ten badges buy one of the next rank, except that nothing trades up into platinum. A single platinum still buys your pick of Sealed J-Sword, Psycho Wand, Heaven Punisher, Handgun: Guld, Prophets of Motav or Sonic Doll.

Nine years of patching

The skeleton was built in 2017: Maximum Attack E, the Platinum Badge and the milestone system all arrived at once, and those three then went unchanged through nine events. What did move was how milestones were counted, and how long badges lasted. Laid out year by year, you can see what the staff kept trying to fix.

YearDatesWhat changed

The "Ephinea Summer Event". Three badge types, and the featured quests were Maximum Attack 4. No milestones yet. Extended mid-event into the only seven-week edition.

20227th6 Aug – 6 Sep

20238th5 Aug – 5 Sep

Fix attempt one. The counter moves to all Ultimate kills, so progress can come from outside the event quests.

20249th12 Aug – 9 Sep

Fix attempt two. The counter moves to clears of the least-cleared quest. Grinding a single quest stops helping.

Fix attempt three. −25% badges for the three most-cleared quests, +25% for the three least. Forest still finished 5.6× ahead.

Sources: the announcement threads for 2016/2017/2018/2019/2020/2021/2022, and the Ephinea PSO Wiki

Three consecutive years of corrections, and the distribution barely moved. Only after that does replacing the quests and the currency outright start to look like the obvious step — which is the order the announcement comes in.

Still unknown

More is missing from the announcement than is in it. Once the event starts, these are the things to check, and they will be written up here.

  • How many new quests, and which areas. Whether "uniform difficulty" actually holds up
  • How many token colours, and what they are called
  • How the real shop prices compare with the examples (item ticket = 2 red + 2 blue, and so on)
  • What the platinum equivalent is called, and how it is obtained
  • Whether the milestone tiers and thresholds changed
