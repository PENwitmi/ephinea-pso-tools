About this calculation

Based on the official Ephinea wiki feeding tables and evolution conditions. It works backwards by fixing the stats at each evolution first, then choosing food. Since stats never decrease, every intermediate state must stay at or below the target — which narrows the candidates to a finite set.

Steps are given as "N of one food". When two foods must be combined to make progress, it shows a ratio like "[A×7 + B×1] ×15 sets" — you never need to alternate one at a time.

When does the character matter?

The form is judged on every single item you feed, against the mag's level at that moment. If the level is not a checkpoint, it makes no difference who feeds it. The flip side: while the mag sits on a checkpoint level, one item fed by a different character is enough to change its form (it changes even if the level does not go up).

So the stretch that needs care is from mag Lv50 until the rare mag locks in. While the level is a multiple of 5 it is re-judged any number of times, so handing it to a character other than the one specified will silently turn it into a different form (one item fed by another character at Lv55 is enough, with no level-up). After the rare mag, no judgement happens at all, so you can hand it to anyone.

Hand the mag between characters through the account-wide common bank. Type /bank in chat to open it, deposit the mag, then withdraw it on another character.

Rare-mag plans are listed first because everything after that point is just feeding, with no evolution to worry about.

Plans are ranked by how little work they are. Work is measured by how many things you have to track, not by how many items you feed: feeding the same item 292 times while watching a number is 1 thing to track, alternating two items for 34 sets is 34, and counting out 292 items with no marker is 292. One hand-over counts as 10 steps (deposit, switch character, withdraw, re-equip are all things you can get wrong, and letting another character hold the mag between mag Lv50 and Lv100 changes its form).
Total items and elapsed time are not used for ranking, but they are shown so you can weigh them yourself.
