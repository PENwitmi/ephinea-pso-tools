About this calculation

Based on the official Ephinea wiki feeding tables and evolution conditions. It works backwards by fixing the stats at each evolution first, then choosing food. Since stats never decrease, every intermediate state must stay at or below the target — which narrows the candidates to a finite set.

Steps are given as "N of one food". When two foods must be combined to make progress, it shows a ratio like "[A×7 + B×1] ×15 sets" — you never need to alternate one at a time.

The third evolution (Lv50) depends on class, the ordering of your stats, and whether the Section ID is even or odd. The fourth evolution (rare mag) happens from Lv100, and once it does the form never changes again. Rare-mag plans are listed first precisely because everything after that point is just feeding, with no evolution to worry about.

Plans are ordered by how little hassle they are: (1) fewest ratio steps, (2) fewest hand-offs, (3) fewest steps, (4) fewest kinds of food. Total item count and time are deliberately not part of the ranking — how many items you feed has almost no bearing on how easy a plan is to follow.

Data sources: Ephinea PSO Wiki (equipment, prices, technique mechanics) / character growth data follows vanilla PSOBB / Inspired by the classic status simulator
